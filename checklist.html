Bali Travel Checklist 2026

Don't get stuck at the airport. Complete these 5 essential steps before you fly to Bali to ensure a smooth entry.

1

Passport & Visa (e-VOA)

Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months and has 1 blank page. Most travelers need a Visa on Arrival (VOA).

Official e-Visa Portal Fee: ~IDR 500,000 ($32)
2

Digital Arrival Card (72h before)

Complete the All Indonesia Digital Arrival Form within 3 days before landing. You will receive a QR code to show at immigration.

Official Arrival Form
3

Bali Tourism Levy

A mandatory contribution for all international visitors to support Bali's culture and environment. Pay online to skip the airport queue.

Love Bali Portal Fee: IDR 150,000 ($10)
4

Proof of Onward Travel

Airlines and immigration strictly require proof of a flight leaving Indonesia within your visa duration (30 or 60 days).

5

Essential Apps & Connectivity

Download these apps to navigate, order food, and book transport easily on the island.

Grab / Gojek
Google Maps
WhatsApp
Airalo (eSIM)

Pro Tips

  • Screenshot all QR codes; airport Wi-Fi can be unreliable.
  • Carry some cash (IDR) for small local shops and drivers.
  • Get an International Driving Permit (IDP) if renting a scooter.

Health & Safety

  • Travel insurance is highly recommended (covers scooter accidents).
  • Avoid tap water; stick to bottled or filtered water.
  • Respect temple dress codes (wear a sarong).
