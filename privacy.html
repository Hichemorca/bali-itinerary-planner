Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

BaliPlan ("we", "us") operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

We collect your quiz answers (trip length, budget, travel style, interests, region) and store them only in your own browser (localStorage) to generate your itinerary. If you optionally provide an email address, we store it to deliver your plan and occasional itinerary updates. Payment processing is handled entirely by Gumroad; we never see your card details.

2. Cookies and analytics

We may use basic analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) with anonymized cookies to understand site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time; the planner works without them.

3. Third-party links

Our itineraries include booking links to GetYourGuide, Viator, and Klook. These platforms have their own privacy policies governing any data you share with them.

4. Data retention & deletion

Email addresses are retained only for the purposes above. To request deletion, email support@baliplan.travel.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@baliplan.travel.
